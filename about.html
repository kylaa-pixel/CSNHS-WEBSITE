Our School

Catalino Soliguen National High School

Catalino Soliguen National High School (CSNHS) is a public secondary school committed to providing quality, accessible education to the youth of our community. Named in honor of the late Catalino Soliguen, a dedicated public servant and education advocate, the school stands as a beacon of learning, progress, and community service.

Guided by the DepEd K-12 Basic Education Program, CSNHS offers complete Junior High School and Senior High School programs — including ABM, TVL, and HUMSS strands — equipping learners with knowledge, skills, and values necessary for higher education, employment, and nation-building.

📜 Brief History

Catalino Soliguen National High School was established to bring quality secondary education closer to the community. Named after the respected public servant Catalino Soliguen, the institution began with a vision to empower local youth through learning. Over the years, it has grown from a small community school into a full-fledged secondary institution offering complete Junior and Senior High School programs.

Today, guided by the leadership of Principal Thelma G. Buga-ay and supported by committed teachers, parents, and stakeholders, CSNHS continues to uphold its founding mission: to produce graduates who are academically competent, morally upright, and socially responsible citizens — living out the school's motto: "Learn Today, Lead Tomorrow."
