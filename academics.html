ACADEMICS

Curriculum & Programs Offered

The K-12 Basic Education Curriculum

We follow the official K-12 Curriculum by the Department of Education — designed to prepare every student for college, career, and life.

📖 Junior High School Program

Grades 7 to 10 — The foundation of basic education. Students study a balanced set of subjects including Mathematics, Science, English, Filipino, Araling Panlipunan, MAPEH, and TLE. This level builds fundamental knowledge, critical thinking skills, and good study habits needed for Senior High School and beyond.

🎓 Senior High School — Tracks Offered

In Grades 11 and 12, students choose a track that matches their interests, skills, and future goals. CSNHS proudly offers:

📚 Academic Track

For college-bound students.

Advanced subjects, research, and specialized fields preparing you for university. Ideal for future teachers, lawyers, accountants, engineers, and medical professionals.

🔧 Tech-Pro Track

Technology + Professional Skills.

Hands-on practical training leading to national certifications. Students gain skills for immediate employment, entrepreneurship, or technology-related college degrees.
