Academic Programs

📖

Junior High School

Grades 7–10 following the K-12 Basic Education Curriculum. Core subjects include Filipino, English, Mathematics, Science, Araling Panlipunan, MAPEH, and Edukasyon sa Pagpapakatao (ESP).

🎓

Senior High School — ABM

Accountancy, Business and Management track. Focuses on financial literacy, business management, and entrepreneurship — preparing students for careers in commerce and finance.

🔧

Senior High School — TVL

Technical-Vocational-Livelihood track. Hands-on skills training in various vocational fields, leading to National Certification (NC) and immediate employment opportunities.

📚

Senior High School — HUMSS

Humanities and Social Sciences track. Explores human behavior, social studies, politics, and communication — ideal for future educators, lawyers, journalists, and public servants.

📋 Grading System & Promotion Guidelines

  • Grading Scale: All subjects use a numerical rating from 60 to 100, with descriptive ratings.
  • Passing Grade: The minimum passing grade per quarter and per subject is 75.
  • Computation: Quarterly grades are based on Written Work, Performance Tasks, and Quarterly Assessment — weighted per subject.
  • General Average: Computed from all subjects; determines honors and promotion.
  • Promotion: Students must pass all subjects to be promoted. Failure in one or more subjects may require remedial classes or retention.
  • Honor Roll: Students with general average of 90 or higher qualify for academic honors.
